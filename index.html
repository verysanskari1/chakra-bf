About the role

We are a small, high-performing team of one looking to grow to two. This is a permanent, full-time position with no fixed end date. You will be the only person in the role and will report directly to the founder. The work is steady, the company is stable, and the founder is, by her own assessment, a delight to work with.

What you'll actually do

  • Show up. Consistently. Physically and emotionally.
  • Communicate. Clearly, kindly, and before things become a problem.
  • Remember the things that matter. The coffee order. The bad day. The mother's birthday.
  • Disagree like an adult. Then resolve it like one.

Important requirements

  • Emotionally available. (Important. This is essentially the whole job.)
  • Replies to messages within one (1) business hour. (Important.)
  • Gainfully employed or building something real.
  • Has friends he sees and a life outside this role.
  • Knows the difference between "your" and "you're." (Not important. But noticed.)

Not a fit if

  • You are currently seeing someone else "casually." We do not do moonlighting here.
  • You are looking for a rebound. This is a permanent role, not a contract gig.
  • You refer to every past partner as "crazy." We will assume the common factor.
